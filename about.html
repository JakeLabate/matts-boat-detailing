Meet the founder

Hello, my name is Matthew Ludden.

I always admired the beauty these vessels can capture. Now I am the one making them glow.

Welcome to Matt's Boat Detailing. I have been detailing professionally for over half a decade, and before that, car detailing was a big hobby of mine. Ever since, I have been meeting wonderful people, learning new techniques, and perfecting my craft.

I am grateful to have learned from some great boat detailing mentors who gave me a chance to show how devoted and hard-working I am about learning all I can in this field. Growing up in Milford, CT, living down the street from the beach and around the corner from the Housatonic River most of my life, I would always see the various boats coming in and out and watch in awe as they glimmered across the water.

The company

Five years of growth and trust.

For over half a decade, MBD Marine Care has been dedicated to one mission: keeping boats looking their absolute best on the water. What started as a passion for restoring and protecting vessels along the Connecticut shoreline has grown into a trusted mobile marine detailing company serving boat owners, marinas, and yacht brokers across the region.

Today we are known for delivering consistent, high-quality results with a focus on professionalism, reliability, and long-lasting protection for every boat we touch. Most of our business comes from repeat clients, referrals, marinas, and boat brokers who trust us to prepare and maintain vessels to a professional standard. That trust is something we take seriously, and it continues to drive everything we do.

What we do

Boats are not just cleaned. They are restored.

Every project is approached with a meticulous eye for detail, so a boat performs and looks its best in a harsh marine environment.

  • Full exterior detailing and oxidation removal
  • Gelcoat restoration and high-speed polishing
  • Ceramic coating for long-term protection
  • Interior deep cleaning and refinishing
  • Teak, metal, and non-skid surface care
  • Seasonal maintenance and upkeep programs
