Where quality is key

Your boat is an investment. We treat every hull like it is our own.

MBD Marine Care serves boat owners, marinas, and yacht brokers up and down the Connecticut coast. Founder Matthew Ludden grew up in Milford, a street from the beach and a corner from the Housatonic, watching vessels shimmer on the water. Now his team is the reason they shine.

We do more than a quick wash. Every project gets a meticulous eye for detail, professional-grade products, and long-lasting protection rather than a temporary shine. Most of our work comes from repeat clients and referrals, and that trust drives everything we do.

Service packages

From a maintenance wash to a three-year ceramic coat.

Pricing is by the foot so you only pay for the boat you have. Larger corrections and specialty work are quoted after a quick assessment.

Wash & Wax

Hand wash, brightwork, isinglass, non-skid, spray wax, and a polymer sealant. Cabin cleaning optional.

$35/ ft

Resurgence

For heavily oxidized boats: sanding, two-step compounding, polish, and sealant to bring the gelcoat back.

Buff & Shine
