Why Stratford is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Housatonic River and the Sound off Short Beach

The west bank of the river is still lined with working marinas, as it has been since the shipyards of the 1700s.

The Housatonic is a tidal river, so boats kept here sit in brackish water that swings between fresh and salt with every cycle. That mix leaves a stubborn stain line right at the waterline, browner and harder to shift than what we see on boats kept out in open salt water.

Wash & Wax in Stratford: The wash is the easy half here. The waterline is where a brackish-water boat needs the time, and we budget for it rather than rinsing past it.
