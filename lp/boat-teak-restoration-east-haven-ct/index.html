Why East Haven is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Farm River estuary and the Sound at Cosey Beach

There is no large commercial marina inside the town line, which is exactly why a mobile detailer belongs here.

Boats here mostly live at home on trailers, on private docks, or in slips over in a neighboring town. A boat parked at the house is not growing anything below the waterline, which saves real money, but it sits under trees, collects pollen and sap in the spring, and picks up bird droppings that will etch gelcoat if they sit through a hot week.

Teak Restoration in East Haven: Teak on a boat stored in the open takes full sun with nothing shading it, and sap from overhanging trees on top of that. Both come out.
