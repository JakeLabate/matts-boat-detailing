Why Guilford is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Guilford Harbor, the West River, and Sachem's Head

The largest boats and the deepest mooring fields we regularly service. Guilford Yacht Club alone handles vessels up to 60 feet.

Guilford has more moored boats than most towns we serve, and a moored boat is a different animal from a slipped one. It swings with wind and tide and gets a full 360 degrees of sun and spray with nothing alongside to shade it. The salt marshes that make the harbor beautiful also make it productive water, and growth at the waterline comes on quickly through midsummer.
