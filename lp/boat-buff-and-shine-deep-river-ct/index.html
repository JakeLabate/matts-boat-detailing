What Deep River water does to a finish.

Broad, calm, and effectively fresh water. Boats here are river boats in the full sense.

Owners often assume a fresh-water boat needs less work than a salt-water one. On the finish, that is not quite true. There is no salt load and very little marine growth, which is a real advantage. But the Connecticut River carries heavy silt and tannins, and tannin staining at the waterline is one of the harder things to remove on this coast. Left alone for a season, a Deep River boat often needs two-step compounding at the waterline.
