Why Greenwich is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Captain Harbor, Cos Cob Harbor, and Byram Harbor

We come here for larger jobs and for owners who book several boats at one club or yard.

The town runs an unusual split. The four resident marinas at Byram, Cos Cob, Grass Island, and Greenwich Point are full of small open boats, capping around 21 to 28 feet, which take full sun on the gelcoat, seats, and non-skid all season. The private clubs and Mianus River yards are the opposite: larger vessels and finishes that are expensive to correct once they go.

Shrink Wrapping & Winterizing in Greenwich: Wrapping over a season of port grit traps abrasive dust against the finish all winter. Getting her washed first is worth more than most owners realize it is.
