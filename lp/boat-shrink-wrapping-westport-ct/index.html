Why Westport is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Saugatuck River, Compo Beach, and the Sound beyond

Boats here tend to stay put once an owner has a spot, which suits a maintenance rhythm.

The town runs its own marinas and there are waiting lists for in-water and dry stall assignments. Boats kept toward Compo and the mouth of the Saugatuck sit in more open water than the river yards upstream. More wind means more salt spray drying on the topsides, and salt left to bake in the sun is what dulls a finish fastest.

Shrink Wrapping & Winterizing in Westport: A boat that has taken salt all season should go under wrap clean rather than dirty, because salt left on gelcoat under plastic for five months does real damage. We wash before we wrap.
