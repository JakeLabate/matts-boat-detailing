Why Stamford is like this

The widest range of boat sizes we deal with in any single town, from Czescik dinghies to 120 footers at Harbor Point.

The hurricane barrier and the branch structure make Stamford Harbor genuinely calm, which owners love and finishes do not. Enclosed water means less wind and rain reaching the boat, so the film that settles out of a busy urban and commercial harbor just sits on the horizontal surfaces rather than getting rinsed off.

Ceramic Coating in Stamford: Coating does not stop the film settling, but it stops it bonding. On a harbor boat that turns a scrubbing job back into a rinsing job.
