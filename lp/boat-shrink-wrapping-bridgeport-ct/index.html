Why Bridgeport is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Black Rock Harbor, Cedar Creek, and Bridgeport Harbor

It is a genuine working port, and that industrial character shows up on your topsides.

Freighters and commercial vessels run up Black Rock Harbor and Cedar Creek to load and discharge cargo. A commercial harbor puts soot, diesel exhaust, and industrial grit into the air, and it settles on horizontal surfaces every night. Owners who keep a boat here often find a gritty film on the deck and house within days of a wash.

Shrink Wrapping & Winterizing in Bridgeport: Wrapping over a season of port grit traps abrasive dust against the finish all winter. Getting her washed first is worth more than most owners realize it is.
