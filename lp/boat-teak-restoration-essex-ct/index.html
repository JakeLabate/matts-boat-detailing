Why Essex is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Connecticut River, Essex Harbor, and North Cove

A serious boatbuilding town with real yard capability, and more wooden and classic boats than anywhere else we work.

This far up the river the water is much closer to fresh than salt, and that changes everything about the waterline. There is very little of the salt growth we deal with down on the Sound. Instead there is silt, tannin staining, and a distinct brown line that sets into gelcoat and needs proper correction rather than a wash.

Teak Restoration in Essex: Essex and the upper river hold more wooden and classic boats than anywhere else we work, and brightwork on a classic is not the same job as gelcoat on a production hull. We will tell you honestly when a piece belongs with a yard's finish carpenter.
