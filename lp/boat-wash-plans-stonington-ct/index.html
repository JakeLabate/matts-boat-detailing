Why Stonington is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Stonington Harbor, Fishers Island Sound, and the Pawcatuck River

Connecticut's easternmost port, the only one with real Atlantic exposure, and home to the state's last commercial fishing fleet.

Everything west of here is Long Island Sound, which is protected water whether owners think of it that way or not. Stonington is not. This harbor gets genuine ocean exposure, and boats kept here take a salt load that nothing on the rest of our coast matches. A finish that would hold two seasons in Milford will look tired in one here. The working fleet adds diesel, fish, and bait residue to the air on top of that.

Wash Plans in Stonington: Nothing on this coast puts salt on a boat the way this harbor does, and a wash cadence is the cheapest way to keep that salt from becoming a correction bill.
