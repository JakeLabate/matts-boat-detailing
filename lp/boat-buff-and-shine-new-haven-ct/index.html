Why New Haven is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

New Haven Harbor, the Quinnipiac River, and Morris Cove

From City Point on the west to Morris Cove on the east and up into Fair Haven.

A boat slipped at City Point and a boat slipped up the Quinnipiac face genuinely different problems. Out on the harbor it is salt, wind, and the airborne fallout that comes with a commercial port. Up the river it is silt, tannins, and a much heavier waterline stain that sets into the gelcoat rather than sitting on top of it.

Buff & Shine in New Haven: Mixed water leaves a waterline stain that is neither purely growth nor purely tannin, and it tends to need proper compounding rather than a wash. That is this job.
