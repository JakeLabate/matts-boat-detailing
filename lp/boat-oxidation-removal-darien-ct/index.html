Why Darien is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Five Mile River, Scotts Cove, and the Sound

The Five Mile River runs barely 1.25 miles and carries eight marinas along its shore.

Moorings line the channel for most of the river's length. A moored boat sits in water that runs between salt and brackish depending on tide and rainfall, and it swings with no dock alongside to shade it. Fading here is even but thorough, and the waterline picks up a stain heavier than a boat kept out in open salt water.
