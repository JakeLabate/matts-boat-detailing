Why East Lyme is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Niantic Bay, the Niantic River, and Smith Cove

A broad bay wide open to the Sound, and an excellent, somewhat overlooked boating area.

The difference between a boat kept out on Niantic Bay and one tucked up in Smith Cove is significant. The bay is genuinely open to the Sound, so bay boats take wind-driven salt spray constantly and the finish dulls faster than owners expect. Smith Cove and the upper river are protected and much calmer, but calm water is also less flushed, so film settles and stays.

Ceramic Coating in East Lyme: Constant wind-driven spray dulls a finish faster than owners expect, and a coating is the only thing that changes that rate rather than just cleaning up after it.
