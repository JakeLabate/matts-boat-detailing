Why New London is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Thames River and New London Harbor

Recreational boats here share the river with vessels many times their size. It has the character of a working port.

A working port with ferries, commercial shipping, and naval traffic puts diesel exhaust and grit into the air, and it settles on horizontal surfaces overnight. That grit is mildly abrasive, and wiping it off dry is exactly how fine scratching gets into a finish. The Thames also carries real current, so the waterline works harder than it would in a sheltered basin.

Ceramic Coating in New London: Coating does not stop the film settling, but it stops it bonding. On a harbor boat that turns a scrubbing job back into a rinsing job.
