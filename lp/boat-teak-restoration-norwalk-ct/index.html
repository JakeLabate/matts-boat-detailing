Why Norwalk is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Norwalk Harbor, the Norwalk River, and the Norwalk Islands

Real marine infrastructure, not just dockage. Norwalk Cove alone runs 400 slips and three Travelifts.

The sixteen Norwalk Islands sit a short run offshore, so boats here go out constantly through the season rather than sitting in the slip waiting for a weekend. More hours on the water means more salt spray drying on the topsides and more UV on the gelcoat. A Norwalk boat that gets one detail a season tends to need compounding by the second or third year.

Teak Restoration in Norwalk: Sun and salt spray are what turn teak gray, and a boat that runs offshore gets a full dose of both. Regular sealing is much cheaper than restoration.
