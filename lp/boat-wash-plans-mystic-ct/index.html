Why Mystic is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the Mystic River and Fishers Island Sound

No other three-mile stretch of water in Connecticut has this many boatyards on it, and people here notice a finish.

The Mystic River is narrow, heavily trafficked, and crossed by two bridges that hold boats up waiting to pass. All that traffic in a confined channel means constant wake, and boats in slips work against fenders and pilings far more than they would in an open harbor. The water is genuinely mixed, fresh coming down and salt pushing up from Fishers Island Sound, which produces a waterline stain that is neither purely growth nor purely tannin.

Wash Plans in Mystic: Whichever water she sits in, the fix is the same: get to it before it sets. That is easier to do on a schedule than to remember.
