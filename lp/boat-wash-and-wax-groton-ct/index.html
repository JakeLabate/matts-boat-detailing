Why Groton is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

the east bank of the Thames, Fishers Island Sound, and Noank

Noank is a working maritime village in a genuinely good position for Fishers Island, Block Island, and Newport.

Fishers Island Sound is more exposed than anything west of here. Groton and Noank boats get used for real passages rather than afternoon runs, and they take weather accordingly. Salt spray on a boat that regularly crosses open water is a different order of problem from spray on a boat that stays inside a harbor.

Wash & Wax in Groton: Salt spray that dries on the topsides and bakes in the sun is what dulls gelcoat, and a wax layer is what stands between the two. On a boat that gets used, this is the single highest-value service we run.
