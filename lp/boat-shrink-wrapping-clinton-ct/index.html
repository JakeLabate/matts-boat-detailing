Why Clinton is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Clinton Harbor and the Hammonasset River

A quiet harbor tucked behind the Hammonasset preserve, holding Cedar Island Marina, an Elite Marina seven years running.

Clinton Harbor sits against a nature preserve, and preserve water is productive water. Growth at the waterline comes on fast through midsummer here, faster than in a busier, more flushed commercial harbor. The Hammonasset River brings silt and organic material down into the harbor too, so the stain runs browner than a pure salt-water boat's.

Shrink Wrapping & Winterizing in Clinton: Anything that grew at the waterline through midsummer is much easier to take off in the fall than in April. Wrap over a clean hull, not a fouled one.
