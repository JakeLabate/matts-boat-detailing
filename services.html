Long-term protection

Ceramic Coating

A ceramic coating creates a sacrificial barrier between your gelcoat or paint and the elements, including harmful UV rays. It prevents oxidation and hazing for stronger, longer-lasting protection, so results last years instead of months and maintenance becomes a breeze.
