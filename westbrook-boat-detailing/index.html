Westbrook, Connecticut

Patchogue River, Duck Island Roads

Westbrook is about forty-five minutes east and is dominated by one facility. Safe Harbor Pilots Point, still called Brewer Pilots Point by most people around here, runs over 800 slips across multiple basins on the Patchogue River, which makes it one of the largest marinas in New England and by a wide margin the largest we service.

Around it sits a working cluster of smaller yards and repair shops. Duck Island Roads offshore gives Westbrook boats a sheltered anchorage that most towns on this coast do not have.

Local conditions

Scale changes how you should book

At a marina with 800 slips, coordinated scheduling is worth real money to everybody. If you and your dock neighbors want work done, book together. We can run several boats in a day at Pilots Point without moving the truck, and that efficiency comes back to you in what the trip costs to justify.

The Patchogue is a tidal river, so Westbrook boats sit in brackish water that swings with every cycle and leaves a waterline stain browner and more stubborn than open salt water produces. Boats that spend their days anchored off Duck Island have the opposite problem: full sun and salt spray from every angle with nothing alongside to shade them. Tell us which kind of boat yours is and we will quote it accordingly.
