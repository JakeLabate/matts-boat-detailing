Long-term protection

Ceramic Coating

A ceramic coating creates a sacrificial barrier between your gelcoat or paint and the elements, including harmful UV rays. It prevents oxidation and hazing for stronger, longer-lasting protection, so results last years instead of months and maintenance becomes a breeze.

$100per foot · from

Full boat detail from the waterline up with a three-year coating. The coating we use is manufactured as a single-layer coating. Bridge boats and smooth hard-top boats are quoted in person.

  • Hand wash
  • Brightwork cleaning
  • Mold and mildew removal
  • Non-skid cleaning
  • One-step polish
  • One layer of coating applied

The same arithmetic we walk owners through, with the assumptions on the table.

Assumptions, stated plainly. Buff and shine at $65 per foot, ceramic coating at $100 per foot lasting three years, both from the published rates on this site. The coated line assumes one coating at the start and a second in year four. How often an uncoated hull actually hazes depends on where the boat is kept and how hard it is used, which is why that is a control rather than a fixed number. This is an illustration of the arithmetic, not a quotation.
