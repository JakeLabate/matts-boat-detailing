We are not affiliated with Spicer's Noank Marina. We are an independent mobile detailer that services boats kept there.

Spicer's Noank Marina is one of the cluster of serious yards in Noank, the working maritime village at the mouth of the Mystic River. Noank has kept its character as a genuine working waterfront rather than becoming purely recreational.

From here Fishers Island Sound opens out east and south, with Block Island and Newport within comfortable range.

Detailing here

What we watch for at this marina.

Exposure is the defining factor at this end of the coast. Boats based in Noank cross real open water regularly, and salt spray drying repeatedly on topsides is what dulls a finish faster than anything else we deal with.

Given the seventy-minute run from Milford, work out here makes most sense as coating jobs, full corrections, or coordinated bookings. If several owners at the same yard go in together, the trip works properly for everyone and the per-boat cost reflects that.
